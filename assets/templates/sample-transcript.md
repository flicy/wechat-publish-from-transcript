# Sample Transcript / 示例逐字稿

## 中文示例

00:00 主持人：今天我们聊一个很具体的问题，普通人怎么用 AI 把一个想法做成可交付产品。很多人以为重点是会不会写代码，其实第一步是把需求说清楚。

03:20 嘉宾：我最近最大的变化是，不再把前端、后端、测试分得那么清楚。我们现在更像 Builder 团队，每个人都可以围绕目标调动 Agent。

07:45 嘉宾：真正有效率的不是让 AI 写一段代码，而是建立研发飞轮。需求输入、源码定位、Coding、验证、上线、反馈，这几个环节要连起来。

12:10 嘉宾：AI 写完代码以后，我不会马上合并。我会让另一个模型做 pre-review，再用 autofix 循环修复。这样能避免同一个模型既当运动员又当裁判。

16:40 嘉宾：很多非技术同学说不清需求，我建议先用语音讲四五分钟，再让 AI 整理成 markdown plan。上下文留痕之后，跑偏也能回到某一版计划。

21:15 嘉宾：如果有平台高光，比如最高留存率或者评论率特别高，我会把它当成信号，但不会直接照搬。最后仍然要回到逐字稿找证据。

## English Sample

00:00 Host: Today we are discussing a concrete question: how can an ordinary person use AI to turn an idea into a deliverable product? Many people think the key is writing code, but the first step is explaining the requirement clearly.

03:20 Guest: My biggest recent change is that I no longer separate frontend, backend, and testing so sharply. We now work more like a Builder team, where everyone can mobilize agents around a goal.

07:45 Guest: Real productivity does not come from asking AI to write a piece of code. It comes from building an R&D flywheel: requirement input, source-code location, coding, validation, release, and feedback.

12:10 Guest: After AI writes code, I do not merge it immediately. I ask another model to run pre-review, then use an autofix loop. This avoids letting the same model be both player and referee.

16:40 Guest: Many non-technical people cannot explain their requirements clearly. I suggest recording four or five minutes of voice first, then asking AI to organize it into a markdown plan. Once context is written down, you can roll back when the work drifts.

21:15 Guest: If the platform marks a highlight, such as highest retention or highest comment rate, I treat it as a signal, but I do not copy it directly. The final article still has to return to transcript evidence.
